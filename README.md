### About

Engineering leader. Twenty-five years in software, twenty of them leading teams,
from embedded systems through carrier grade network software to cloud platforms.

Most of my work has been platform and infrastructure: the layer everyone else
builds on. Systems that need to scale, but also stay simple enough to evolve.
On the leadership side, years spent helping teams make better technical
decisions and build software that lasts.

This account is the hands-on half. Agent systems, small tools, electronics, and
experiments. I write about what actually breaks at [nunorralves.pt](https://www.nunorralves.pt).
